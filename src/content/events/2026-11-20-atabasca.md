A trio from Abruzzo with twenty years of music behind them, ready to take you on a hypnotic instrumental journey. On their self-titled debut album, released by Rome’s Killer Groove Records, they weave together desert psych, groove and cinematic atmospheres with a spaghetti western flavour. Enveloping rhythms and analogue warmth for an evening full of groove.
