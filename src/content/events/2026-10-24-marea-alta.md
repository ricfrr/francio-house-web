Marea Alta play songs that sound like a summer you half remember: chorus pedals, nylon guitars,
a drum machine that someone found at a flea market in Pescara, and two voices that never quite agree on the melody.

They're coming back to the house to try out the songs from their second record, *Acqua Alta*, before it goes to the
pressing plant. Expect long outros, a cover you won't recognise until the last chorus, and — if the neighbours allow it — an encore on the balcony.

**Before and after:** Gufo Blu spins records from the bossa nova and Italo-library shelves.

Bring something to drink. Leave your shoes on — the floor is terrazzo, it can take it.
