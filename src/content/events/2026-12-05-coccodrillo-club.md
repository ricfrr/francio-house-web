Coccodrillo Club named themselves after the crocodile painted on the outside wall of the house — they saw it
while loading in for their first gig here in 2025 and never changed the name.

Angular guitars, polyrhythms borrowed from Fela records, a bass player who refuses to stand still.
This one will be **loud**: earplugs at the door, for free.
