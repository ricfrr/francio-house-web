Autumn Electric Colors are Alessandro and Elena: hypnotic psychedelia from the Marche, with Syd Barrett and early Pink Floyd as a starting point.
They released an album on Dolmen Records in April 2024; their records are on [Bandcamp](https://ae2020music.bandcamp.com/).
